SONAR MAZE

FIVE LEVELS · TOTAL DARKNESS

  • The maze is invisible. Fire a sonar pulse to light up the walls it touches.
  • Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. Pulse with Space. On a phone, use the stick and tap anywhere else to pulse.
  • A pulse freezes every enemy it reaches for 2 seconds. Frozen enemies are harmless — walk straight through them.
  • Touching an active enemy ends the run. The green exit always glows; batteries and radar pickups only appear inside a pulse.
  • Some walls slide open and shut. Time your run.
  • You start with 7 pulses and gain 5 per level. Finish fast — leftover pulses and batteries become bonus points.
