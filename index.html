SONAR MAZE

Navigate by sound. Trust the dark.

  • The maze is pitch black. Press Space (or tap the screen) to send a sonar pulse that briefly reveals nearby walls.
  • Move with WASD / arrow keys, or the on-screen stick on mobile.
  • A pulse freezes any entity it touches for a couple of seconds — walk through frozen entities safely. Touching an active one ends the run.
  • Find batteries (🔋) and the rare radar pulse (📡) with your sonar to restock and get a free area-wide scan.
  • Some walls shift open and closed over time — you'll only see this while they're lit by a pulse.
  • Reach the pulsating green exit. Five levels, always a way through. You have 7 pulses to start — spend them wisely.
