SONAR MAZE

FIVE LEVELS  ·  ZERO LIGHT
◈The maze is invisible. Send a sonar ping to reveal the walls around you for a few seconds.
↑Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, use the stick in the bottom-left corner.
◉Ping with Space, or tap anywhere outside the stick. Pings are limited — you start with 7 and gain 5 each level.
✦A ping freezes any enemy it touches for 2 seconds. Walk straight through a frozen enemy; touching an active one ends the run.
▤Some corridors are sliding doors that open and close. Time your run through them.
🔋Batteries add 3 pings, radar packs fire one huge pulse. Both stay hidden until a ping finds them.
▲Reach the green exit. The clock never stops — leftover pings and batteries become bonus points.
