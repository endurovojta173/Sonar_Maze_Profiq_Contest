Sonar Maze

Navigate through darkness

🎯 Goal: Find the pulsing green exit across 5 levels.
🔊 Sonar: Press SPACE to send a sonar wave that reveals walls.
👾 Enemies: Red entities kill on contact. Sonar stuns them for 2s.
🔋 Batteries: Hidden power-ups — sonar reveals them.
📡 Super-Ping: Massive sonar blast revealed by your ping.
⚡ Moving walls: Some walls shift open and closed — time your path!
🕹️ Controls: WASD / Arrows to move. SPACE to ping.
📱 Mobile: Virtual joystick + tap to ping.
